Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the UDAAN website (udaan.org.np) collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: we do not use tracking cookies or analytics, and we only use your details to respond to you.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

The website is run by the Nepal Unmanned Aircraft (Drone) Association, UDAAN (नेपाल मानव रहित विमान (ड्रोन) संघ), registered under the Sanstha Darta Ain, 2034 (Reg. No. 207). In this policy, “UDAAN”, “we” and “us” mean the association.

When you visit the website

The website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or social media pixels.

Like almost every website, our hosting server keeps short technical logs of requests, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. These logs are used only to keep the website secure and working.

Services that load on our pages

Our pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts. To do this, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The website does not load Facebook, LinkedIn, X or WhatsApp code. Our share and follow links only open those services when you click them, and their own privacy policies then apply.

When you contact us

If you email, phone or write to us, or send a message through our contact form when it is available, we receive the details you give us, such as your name, email address, phone number and message.

We use these details only to answer you and to keep a record of our correspondence. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Membership and event registration

Membership registration and member login take place on our separate member portal, member.udaan.org.np, which handles the information you submit there.

Some event registrations use Google Forms. Information you enter there is collected through Google and used by UDAAN to organise that event.

Sharing and keeping your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed to run an activity you signed up for, or when the law requires it.

We keep correspondence and registration details only for as long as we need them for the purpose they were given, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing . We will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
