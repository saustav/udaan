A Founding Supporter of UDAAN

Mr. Dinesh DC served as Chairman of the Film Development Board, Nepal from 2081 to 2083 BS. During his tenure, FDB gave UDAAN the platform and opportunity that helped pave the way for the association's formation.

FDB has remained one of UDAAN's closest partners since, from hosting the national panel discussion Shaping the Sky to the FDB 10-Day Drone Training, which UDAAN technically supervised for 25 trainees.

“Thank you for providing us with the platform and opportunity that helped pave the way for the formation of UDAAN. Your trust and guidance have been instrumental in bringing our vision to life.”

— Plaque of Appreciation, Raj Bhai Suwal, President, and the UDAAN Family

UDAAN thanks Mr. DC for his meaningful contribution to the growth of Nepal's unmanned aviation community and wishes him every success ahead.
