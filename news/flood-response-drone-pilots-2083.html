Bhotekoshi–Trishuli Floods, Bhadra 2083

On Bhadra 10, 2083 (26 August 2026), destructive floods on the Bhotekoshi and Trishuli rivers struck Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Chitwan and nearby districts, causing heavy loss of life and property. Helicopters from the Government of Nepal, the Nepal Army and the private sector flew continuous rescue and relief missions.

The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority (NDRRMA) issued a special request to helicopter and drone associations to support search and rescue, in coordination with District Administration Offices, security agencies and local governments. This builds on UDAAN's MoU with NDRRMA for disaster management and emergency rescue.

Our Thanks to the Pilots

Thank you to every drone pilot for your unwavering courage, technical mastery, and absolute focus under extreme pressure. Your work showed what responsible, coordinated drone operations can do for Nepal in an emergency.

Official Notices from NDRRMA

NDRRMA's second notice warned that some individuals and organisations were flying drones without prior approval from local administration, creating serious risks for rescue helicopters. Under the Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (RPA/Drone) Flight Procedure, 2075, drones may be flown only with prior approval from the relevant District Administration Office or the Ministry of Home Affairs, and violations face legal action.

warning Safety Reminder for Pilots

  • Never fly in a disaster or rescue zone without prior approval from the District Administration Office or the Ministry of Home Affairs.
  • Coordinate with local authorities and security agencies before and during any mission.
  • Always give way to rescue helicopters and other manned aircraft.
  • Follow official updates at bipad.gov.np.
