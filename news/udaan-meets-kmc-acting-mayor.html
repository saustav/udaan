Joining Hands on Drone Technology

The UDAAN team visited the Kathmandu Metropolitan City (KMC) office for a meeting with Acting Mayor Sunita Dangol. The conversation centred on how Nepal's unmanned aircraft community can support the city, and how UDAAN and KMC can work hand in hand on drone technology.
