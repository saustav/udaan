A 400-Fold Fee Increase Without Consultation

The Ministry of Culture, Tourism and Civil Aviation raised the drone registration fee by about 400 times. In a formal letter (Ref. No. 03), UDAAN told the Ministry it is seriously concerned, both about the size of the increase and that such an important policy decision was made without informing or consulting stakeholders.

Concerns Raised

  • trending_up An increase of about 400 times, decided without informing or consulting the organisations affected
  • flight Drones play an important role in tourism promotion, cultural documentation, disaster management, research and other development work
  • groups A sudden increase this large discourages Nepali startups, researchers, creative young people and businesses working in the sector
  • public The same fee for Nepali and foreign nationals undermines equal access and a fair, competitive environment

What UDAAN Asked For

  • help Clear information on the reasons for the increase, the process followed and its long-term objectives
  • forum A meeting with the Ministry to discuss the issue directly
  • handshake Stakeholder participation as a required part of making policy like this

— UDAAN letter to the Ministry, 13 April 2025
